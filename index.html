THE METHOD

Fat loss without losing your strength

Your previous 67 kg result shows you have experience with consistent training. Treat it as a meaningful past milestone, not a deadline. Your current starting point and health matter more than repeating the old pace.

  • Aim for a modest calorie deficit; avoid crash diets.
  • Include protein at every meal and plenty of vegetables, fruit and fibre.
  • Keep carbohydrates around training: rice, potatoes, oats, bread or fruit are useful fuel.
  • Sleep 7–9 hours where possible. Take recovery days seriously.
  • Track weekly average weight and waist; a sensible rate is often around 0.25–0.75 kg/week, but individual results vary.

Training rules

  • Warm up 5–10 minutes before strength, intervals or running.
  • Most strength sets should finish with 1–3 good reps still possible. Start kettlebell movements light and master technique before increasing load.
  • Increase only one thing at a time: reps, sets, kettlebell load or distance. Choose a bell you can control for every rep.
  • Do not add extra hard burpee/jump-rope/kettlebell sessions on top of the plan. Kettlebell swings are optional technique-dependent work, not a conditioning test.
  • Stop for sharp pain, chest pain, faintness or unusual breathlessness; seek medical advice when appropriate.

Equipment checklist

Pull-up bar · resistance bands · one suitable kettlebell · jump rope · running shoes · stationary bike · water · safe floor space.
